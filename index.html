Search tips
  • Several words must all match: volute 12x8
  • Paste a list of LWH IDs, Customer IDs, INV receipts, item numbers or bays (one per line, or separated by spaces or commas). Anything not found is listed.
  • Field filters: item: bay: id: (LWH ID) cust: (Customer ID) rcv: (INV receipt) ref: lot: desc:
  • Wildcards: bay:E0* finds bays E01 to E09. Age: age>180 or age<30 (days)
  • Quotes keep an item number with spaces together: "SPHFGS6 000 X"
